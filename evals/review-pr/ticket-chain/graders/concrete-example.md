PASS if the breakfast-per-room problem is illustrated with a concrete case: a
specific quote whose room lines differ on breakfast, such as "the Doubles
with breakfast and the Singles without" or "10 Doubles, 4 of them with
breakfast", and why the hotel or the PMS can't take that split. Naming the
room lines that differ is enough; counts and prices are not required.

FAIL if the problem is only explained in the abstract ("breakfast should be
per quote, not per room") with no specific quote.
